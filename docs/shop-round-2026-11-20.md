_20 November 2026. Everything below is generated by `cd commerce && npm run site`
from `catalogue/products.csv`. Nothing is hand-edited in `dist/`._

This round answered five things: extra product-connection routes and their
commission, SEO + AEO + GEO, a name, the marketplace taxonomy, and a home page
built to convert. Each answer is code, not a paragraph about code.

## 1. The name, and what it commits us to

**Exommeece** — `https://exommeece.co.uk`. Twelve departments, GBP, UK-only
shipping, VAT-inclusive prices, free delivery over £40, 30-day free returns.

The name is a placeholder in one sense only: it is invented, and a trademark
search has not been done. Everything else about it is real — the domain, the
department structure, the delivery promise, the schema, and the feed.

Where it lives: [`catalogue/site.ts`](../catalogue/site.ts) — `BRAND`, `DEPARTMENTS`,
`DEPARTMENT_ALIASES`, `SEARCH_SYNONYMS`. Change the brand there and 50 pages,
the feed, `llms.txt` and every page title change with it.

## 3. The four product-source routes (the short version)

| Route | What you are paid | The catch that decides it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alibaba** | Nothing. You buy at trade price and keep the margin | Minimum order quantities, freight, duty and import VAT — a single-unit order lands above retail |
| **AliExpress Portals** | 1.8–5.4% in the UK, 3-day cookie, **US$50 cap per sale** | The cap turns 5.4% into 4% at £900 and 2% at £2,000; long delivery means more returns, and returns are deducted |
| **Amazon UK Associates** | 3–4.5% on home, **24-hour cookie** | The Product Advertising API needs **3 qualifying sales in 180 days** — so the catalogue cannot be built from Amazon's data until Amazon has been sold for |
| **eBay Partner Network** | 1–4%, 24-hour cookie, US$100–550 per-transaction cap | The 7.5% Ambassador rate is invite-only; the open programme is the weakest of the three for considered purchases |

The decision, in one function:

```ts
routeFor({ averageOrder: money(12000), markupMultiple: 3.4, hasFreeTraffic: true })
// → "resell": a £60+ basket at a 3× markup carries carriage, fees and returns and still leaves a margin
routeFor({ averageOrder: money(4999), markupMultiple: 3.2, hasFreeTraffic: true })
// → "refer": under a £60 basket the affiliate commission beats the resale net, with no stock risk
```

On the demo basket the numbers are close enough to be the whole argument:
**£3.99 as a referral vs £3.71 as a reseller** on the same £49.99 sale — 28p for
taking on stock, returns and carriage. At £180 of garden furniture the resale
margin wins, because the referral is capped.

## 4. What the shopper sees, and why each bit is there

- **Price includes VAT, delivery cost on the card** — the two facts that most
  often appear at the last step elsewhere. `deliveryLine()` puts "+ £3.95 delivery"
  or "Free delivery" on every card before a click.
- **"In stock — dispatched today"** comes from the supplier file, not a guess. Out
  of stock shows the price, disables the basket, and offers a mailto instead of
  taking an order it cannot fill.
- **"Only 3 left"** where the file says three. No countdown timers anywhere.
- **Reductions against a price the catalogue used to charge**, labelled with the
  percentage, and no fake scarcity around them.
- **Key facts table** — material, dimensions, weight, care, department, delivery,
  returns — above the long description on every product page.
- **Basket** — quantity stepper, per-line ± and remove, a free-delivery progress
  bar, and a checkout button that says "not connected" because it is not.
- **FAQs written as answers** — 5 on the home page, 3 per stocked department,
  with `FAQPage` markup behind them.

## 6. What is still not done

1. **No checkout.** Basket only. `GAPS.md` item 1, unchanged.
2. **Affiliate links are not wired.** The rates are data; there is no cloaking,
   click logging or attribution. Until there is, the catalogue is a shop that
   cannot take money and does not earn commission either.
3. **Placeholder imagery.** 33 generated SVGs, labelled as placeholders in the
   alt text. Real photography is the largest single visual improvement available.
4. **The copy is mine, not a copywriter's.** Honest, but it is one voice across
   32 products.
5. **No reviews, no facets, no order emails.** See `GAPS.md`.
6. **Deploy not done.** `catalogue/dist` is ready for any static host;
   the domain, DNS and `SITE_URL` are the user's to set.
